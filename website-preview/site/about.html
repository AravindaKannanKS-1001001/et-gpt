About Us
Our Company.

Founded in 2004, Earth Tekniks is a leading solution provider, specialized in designing and building solutions for manufacturing industries to support them in accomplishing their quality motto and business goals. With the continuously evolving technology landscape, we believe progressive transformation is fundamental for business success. At Earth Tekniks, we thrive on innovation to craft and deliver the best-fitting solution that will help our customers to mitigate challenges and ensure high-quality of products in the most-efficient and cost-effective way. We have a dedicated R&D centre backed by a strong team of experts to design, develop and deliver custom hardware and software modules to suit the unique requirements of every manufacturing set-up.
