ET-GPT

Embeddable AI support assistant for EarthTekniks — requirements overview (Phase 0)

What we are building

Chat widget

Floating launcher, expandable panel, streaming answers, history, mic and attachments. Drops into the mock site, then production, with two settings: origin and widget ID.

Smart assistant

Understands company questions, optics calculations and catalog lookups, and chains them: knowledge → calculation → catalog. Asks when unsure, never guesses.

Safe by design

Human approval for every calculation, PII redaction, scope guardrails, rate and time limits, per-user data isolation.

Admin insights

Separate dashboard: questions, trends, common issues, unanswered queries, usage and improvement ideas.

How a request flows

Message→Redact PII→Guardrails→Classify (OpenJEV)→Plan→MCP tools→Approval form→Answer

Risks we are watching

  • OpenJEV is a community-funded third-party service; a fallback router is planned and it only ever sees redacted text.
  • Nemotron may not read images; a free NVIDIA vision model is the fallback.
  • PII redaction is never perfect; we will publish a measured recall number.
  • Anonymous guests can be abused; IP and session limits apply.
