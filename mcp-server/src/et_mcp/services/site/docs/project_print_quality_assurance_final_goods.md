# Print Quality Assurance in Final Goods

## Project Overview

The Print Quality Assurance System is a machine vision solution designed for verification of printed information on finished products before they reach the market.

The system combines Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and barcode reading technologies to verify printed product information, detect labeling errors, and prevent non-conforming products from entering distribution channels.

The solution supports quality assurance, traceability, regulatory compliance, and automated rejection of products that fail inspection requirements.

## Inspection Workflow

### Step 1: Image Acquisition

The machine vision system captures images of product labels or packaging.

### Step 2: OCR Processing

The OCR engine extracts textual information from the captured images.

### Step 3: Barcode Decoding

Barcode-reading tools decode available 1D and 2D barcodes.

### Step 4: Information Verification

Extracted information is verified against predefined quality requirements.

### Step 5: Inspection Decision

Products are classified as conforming or non-conforming.

### Step 6: Product Handling

Conforming products continue through the production process.

Non-conforming products are automatically rejected.
