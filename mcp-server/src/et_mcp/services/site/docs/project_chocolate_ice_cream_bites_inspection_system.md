# Chocolate Ice Cream Bites Inspection System

## Project Overview

The Chocolate Ice Cream Bites Inspection System is an automated inspection solution designed for packaging-line quality control.

The system detects broken or missing products before packaging using high-speed infrared (IR) imaging and automated rejection mechanisms.

Products moving on a conveyor are inspected in real time to verify conformity with predefined quality requirements.

---

## Inspection Objectives

The system is designed to identify:

### Product Integrity Defects

- Broken products
- Missing products

### Packaging-Line Quality Verification

- Verification that products meet defined quality criteria before packaging

---

## Inspection Workflow

### Image Acquisition

Products move along a conveyor where they are inspected using a high-speed infrared (IR) camera.

### Illumination

An infrared (IR) light source provides uniform illumination to support image capture.

### Image Processing

Captured IR images are processed by a high-speed image controller to determine product conformity.

### Defect Identification

Products are evaluated against predefined inspection parameters.

Non-conforming products are identified during production.

### Automated Rejection

When a defective product is detected, a high-pressure air nozzle removes the product from the production line.

## Operational Benefits

The inspection system helps ensure that only products meeting quality requirements proceed to packaging.
