# Earth Tekniks

## Company Overview

Earth Tekniks was established in 2004.

The company focuses on providing automation solutions for industrial organizations with the objective of optimizing processes and reducing operational costs.

Earth Tekniks serves customers across multiple manufacturing sectors and has developed domain expertise through projects in:

- Automotive
- Steel
- Chemical
- Pharmaceutical (Pharma)
- Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG)
- Glass
- Other manufacturing industries

The company emphasizes domain expertise, dedicated support, and nationwide customer engagement.

## Solution Areas

Earth Tekniks provides automation and inspection solutions for industrial applications.

### Conveyor Systems

Automation solutions involving conveyor-based industrial systems.

### Industrial Vision Inspection

Machine vision and inspection solutions used for quality assurance and manufacturing validation.

Capabilities include:

#### Optical Character Recognition and Verification (OCR/OCV)

- Optical Character Recognition (OCR)
- Optical Character Verification (OCV)

#### Defect Detection

Automated identification of product defects during manufacturing or inspection processes.

#### Part Verification

Verification of:

- Part orientation
- Functional characteristics
- Dimensions
- Finished-part features

#### Presence and Absence Detection

Detection of whether required components or features are present or missing.

#### Counting Systems

Counting systems designed for bagging machine applications.
