# Ampoule Bottle Inspection System

## Project Overview

The Ampoule Bottle Inspection System is an industrial vision inspection solution designed for automated quality inspection of ampoule bottles during manufacturing.

The system evaluates multiple quality parameters and enables process control by performing automated product inspections throughout the production process.

A customized inspection solution is developed using a combination of:

- Area Scan Cameras
- Line Scan Cameras
- Mechanical handling systems
- Servo drive mechanisms
- ET Vision X Pro software

---

## Inspection Objectives

The system inspects ampoule bottles for the following quality parameters:

### Dimensional Inspection

- Ampoule eccentricity
- Tip diameter
- Ampoule length
- Dot diameter

### Print and Marking Inspection

- Print inspection
- Cut mark inspection in the ring

### Positional Verification

- Distance between the line and the dots

---

## Inspection Technologies

### Area Scan Inspection

Area Scan Cameras are used for dimensional measurements and geometric verification.

Inspection functions include:

- Eccentricity measurement
- Diameter measurement
- Length measurement

### Line Scan Inspection

Line Scan Cameras are used for inspection of round-surfaced objects.

To obtain a complete view of the ampoule:

1. The ampoule is rotated using a mechanical handling system.
2. A servo drive mechanism controls rotation.
3. The Line Scan Camera captures the full surface of the product during rotation.

This approach enables complete surface inspection of the ampoule.

## Functional Capabilities

### Quality Inspection

The system performs automated inspection of ampoule bottles against predefined quality parameters.

### Surface Inspection

Line Scan Cameras combined with controlled rotation enable full-surface inspection of cylindrical ampoules.

### Dimensional Verification

Area Scan Cameras perform measurements of critical dimensions and geometric characteristics.

### Process Control

The system supports tighter control of manufacturing quality by continuously monitoring product compliance during production.
