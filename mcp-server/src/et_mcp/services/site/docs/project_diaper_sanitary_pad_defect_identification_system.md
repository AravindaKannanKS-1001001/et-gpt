# Defect Identification System for Diapers and Sanitary Pads

## Project Overview

The Defect Identification System is a machine vision solution based on the ET Xpro platform, designed for quality inspection of diapers and disposable sanitary napkins during manufacturing.

The system supports multiple inspection applications to detect product defects, verify critical product features, and improve product quality before products reach the market.

The solution is intended to help manufacturers maintain production standards, reduce defects, and prevent customer complaints.

# Surge Detection System

## Overview

The Surge Detection System performs online inspection of the surge layer in disposable sanitary napkins and diapers.

The surge layer is positioned above the liner and forms a primary material layer within the product.

---

## Inspection Objectives

The system verifies:

- Surge layer presence
- Surge layer absence
- Surge layer folding defects

---

## Quality Impact

The source material states that surge layer defects can result in adhesive exposure between the surge layer and liner material.

---

## Inspection Characteristics

### Inspection Length

- Approximately 350 mm inspection length

### Accuracy

The source material states:

- 99.99% inspection accuracy

---

## Hardware Components

### Camera

- 2 Megapixel Monochrome Camera

### Illumination

- High-intensity LED backlight illumination

---

## Functional Benefits

- Online inspection of surge layers
- Detection of folding defects
- Detection of missing surge layers
- Improved product quality

# Liner Opening Detection System

## Overview

The Liner Opening Detection System identifies liner-opening defects in disposable sanitary napkins and diapers.

---

## Inspection Objectives

The system verifies:

- Presence of liner openings
- Correct liner opening formation

---

## Quality Impact

The source material states that failure to detect liner-opening defects may result in pulp material emerging from the product.

The pulp layer functions as a moisture-absorbent material within the product.

---

## Inspection Characteristics

The system is described as a customized high-speed online vision inspection solution.

---

## Hardware Components

### Camera

- 2 Megapixel Monochrome Camera

### Illumination

- Multi-angle high-intensity LED illumination

### Backlight Usage

Backlight illumination is used to acquire shadow images for image processing.

---

## Functional Benefits

- Detection of liner-opening defects
- Improved product quality
- Support for production standards
