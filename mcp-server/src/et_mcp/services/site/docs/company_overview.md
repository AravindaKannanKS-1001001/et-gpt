# Earth Tekniks

## Company Overview

Earth Tekniks was founded in 2004.

The company specializes in designing and building solutions for manufacturing industries. Its solutions are intended to help manufacturers achieve quality objectives and business goals while improving operational efficiency and cost effectiveness.

Earth Tekniks focuses on delivering automation, inspection, and manufacturing support solutions that help customers:

- Improve product quality
- Address manufacturing and inspection challenges
- Increase operational efficiency
- Reduce costs
- Support business objectives

The company maintains a dedicated Research and Development (R&D) center supported by a team of experts responsible for designing, developing, and delivering custom hardware and software modules.

These solutions are tailored to the specific requirements of individual manufacturing environments.
