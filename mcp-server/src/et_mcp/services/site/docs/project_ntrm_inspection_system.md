# Non-Tobacco Related Material (NTRM) Inspection System

## Project Overview

The Non-Tobacco Related Material (NTRM) Inspection System is a machine vision solution designed for tobacco manufacturing environments.

The system detects and removes foreign materials from the tobacco stream before they enter the cigarette-making process.

The objective is to maintain cigarette purity and prevent non-tobacco materials from being incorporated into finished products.

---

## Application Domain

### Industry

Tobacco Manufacturing

### Production Stage

Cigarette Maker Feed Stream

### Inspection Target

Tobacco Material Flow

### Inspection Purpose

- Detection of foreign materials
- Removal of non-tobacco materials
- Product purity assurance
- Manufacturing quality control

---

## Inspection Objectives

The system is designed to:

- Inspect the tobacco stream continuously
- Detect non-tobacco related materials (NTRM)
- Identify foreign particles
- Support removal of detected contaminants
- Improve product reliability
- Maintain product purity

---

## System Architecture

### Camera System

The system uses:

- 4 high-speed cameras

#### Function

The cameras inspect the entire tobacco stream and capture images for foreign material detection.

---

### Controller Unit

#### Function

The Controller Unit:

- Processes images received from the camera system
- Identifies non-tobacco related materials
- Determines the presence of foreign particles

---

### Industrial PC

#### Function

The Industrial PC is used for:

- Real-time monitoring
- Display of inspection information
- Data logging

The source material states that inspection data processed by the PLC is displayed through the Industrial PC.

---

### Protective Enclosure

#### Acrylic Enclosure

The inspection system is enclosed within an acrylic housing.

#### Purpose

The enclosure is intended to:

- Prevent tobacco flakes from obstructing camera views
- Maintain inspection visibility
- Improve image acquisition reliability

---

## Inspection Workflow

### Step 1: Tobacco Stream Monitoring

The tobacco stream passes through the inspection zone.

### Step 2: Image Acquisition

Four high-speed cameras continuously capture images of the tobacco material.

### Step 3: Image Analysis

The Controller Unit analyzes captured images.

### Step 4: Foreign Material Identification

The system identifies materials that do not belong in the tobacco stream.

### Step 5: Monitoring and Logging

Inspection information is displayed and logged through the Industrial PC.

### Step 6: Foreign Material Removal

The source content states that the system is designed to detect and remove foreign particles. Specific rejection hardware or removal mechanisms are not described.
