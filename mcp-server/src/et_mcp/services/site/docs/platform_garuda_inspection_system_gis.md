# Garuda Inspection System (GIS)

## Platform Overview

The Garuda Inspection System (GIS) is an automated bottle inspection solution designed for quality control in bottle manufacturing and blow molding operations.

The system combines machine vision, multi-angle imaging, real-time image processing, automated defect detection, and automated rejection mechanisms to inspect bottles during production.

GIS is designed to support high-speed production environments while maintaining product quality and reducing defective output.

## Defect Categories

### Shape Defects

#### Shape Variation

Detection of deviations from expected bottle shape, including:

- Contour irregularities
- Body symmetry issues
- Neck alignment issues

#### Ovality

Detection of deviations from a circular bottle shape.

---

### Blow Molding Defects

#### Incomplete Blow

Detection of bottles that have not been fully expanded during blow molding.

#### Under/Short Mold

Detection of bottles that have not been adequately filled during molding.

---

### Surface Defects

#### Black Spots

Detection of black spots present on bottle surfaces.

#### Burn Spots

Detection of heat-related defects caused during molding.

#### Flashes and Excess Material

Detection of:

- Flash formation
- Surface protrusions
- Excess molding material

#### Extra Material

Detection of abnormal material accumulation on the bottle structure.

---

### Color Defects

#### Color Variation

Detection of variations in:

- Color intensity
- Color uniformity
- Color hue

---

### Contamination Defects

#### Contamination

Detection of foreign materials including:

- Dust
- Fibers
- Residue

---

### Dimensional Defects

#### Diameter Variation

Detection of dimensional variation along the bottle body.

## Inspection Workflow

### Step 1: Bottle Pick-Up

Freshly blown bottles are picked up from the blow molding machine conveyor using a clamp conveyor.

### Step 2: Transfer to Flat Belt Conveyor

The bottles are transferred to a flat belt conveyor for inspection.

### Step 3: Multi-Angle Image Capture

Multiple high-speed cameras capture images from:

- All sides
- Top view
- Bottom view

### Step 4: Real-Time Image Processing

Captured images are processed in real time.

The system:

- Analyzes images
- Detects anomalies
- Classifies defects

### Step 5: Defect Identification

Detected defects are automatically highlighted.

### Step 6: Pneumatic Ejection

When a bottle fails inspection, a pneumatic ejection mechanism removes it from the production line.

### Step 7: Continuous Monitoring

Operators can monitor inspection performance and make adjustments if required.

### Step 8: Reporting and Analysis

Inspection results are recorded and made available for reporting and analysis.
